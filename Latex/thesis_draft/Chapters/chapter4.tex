\chapter{Conclusions}
\label{ch:conclusions}

This project preparation report has introduced the problem to be studied and the motivations behind it. In Section~\ref{sec:state_of_the_art} it has presented a comprehensive review on the state of the art on modelling and simulating UAVs, as well as the kind of UAV flight controllers used in interception and defense scenarios, and on reachability analysis. Finally, in Chapter~\ref{ch:project_planning} it outlined the tasks the project will consist of, the planned schedule and prior work done. \\

When it comes to the UAV interception problem, the literature reveals that there is a gap in research of reachability analysis-based controllers. This study aims to address that gap, and design an effective and efficient reachability analysis-based controller with practical applications, such that it can provide a UAV with missile defense capabilities with early success guarantees, as well as other advantages granted by reachability analysis.
